# The deck lens

2026-08-30. Landed.

## What was built

`src/ui/deck/` draws what is coming and what is stuck, from the document and
nothing else.

- `deck-groups.ts` - the fold: which pile an item belongs in, and what its
  blockers actually are. Pure, and separate from the components so the argument
  can be read on its own.
- `deck-row.tsx` - one row, shared by every pile, so a queued item and a held one
  cannot drift into looking like different species of thing.
- `deck-lens.tsx` - the piles in order, and what the lens says when it has no
  rows to draw.

Four piles, in this order: **waiting on a person**, **blocked**, **queued**,
**in flight**. Empty piles are not drawn.

One line changed outside the directory - `shell.tsx` hands the lens `nowMs` and
the fleet's work items - plus the deck's own assertions in `tests/panel.test.ts`
and a new `describe` block covering the piles, the held row, both directions of
the blocker rule, and an unreadable deck.

## Decision log

**What waits on a person is drawn first, and `actionable` decides what leads
inside it.** A decision nobody knows is pending is the failure this lens exists
to prevent. `DeckItem.actionable` is upstream's own fold and is carried, never
recomputed - the document type says so, and two implementations of that rule
would disagree the day it changes.

**A deferral is a date, not an age.** An item deferred to a date is still
waiting on a person, so it stays in the first pile, but it does not get the
accent an answerable one does. Colouring a deferred hold as urgent is exactly
the lie the lens is meant to remove. An item can be both - upstream calls
`wi-tidewater-126` actionable and deferred at once - and it leads its pile while
saying plainly that it is deferred.

**The deck lens is handed the fleet's work items.** A blocker arrives as a bare
identity, and the work it names has usually already started, so the deck alone
can neither name it nor tell whether it is still in the way. See
`docs/decisions/2026-08-30-a-blocker-is-an-identity.md` for the rule and its
cost.

**No affordance for answering a held decision.** The next wave grows the ability
to answer one from the page. The held row's title line is a flex row with room
at its end for that control, and there is nothing in it today: a disabled button
is worse than no button, because it invites a click and then explains itself.

**Ages come from the shared `ago` helper**, computed on the server against the
`nowMs` the composition point chose, so every age on the page agrees and nothing
in the browser drifts out of step with it.

## The gap this found

**`DeckItem` carries no `kind`.** The brief asked queued work to show whether it
is research or build. `Worker.kind` exists for the fleet; the deck's records
carry none, and neither does upstream's `backlog` - `contract.ts` parses what
upstream sends, and the field is not in it. Adding it means upstream carrying a
kind per backlog record, `SnapshotRecord` parsing it, `DeckItem` gaining the
field and `DOCUMENT_VERSION` moving - which is the real-fleet-source worker's
territory, and the rule `docs/contract.md` states for it: add the field when a
lens needs it. A lens now needs it.
